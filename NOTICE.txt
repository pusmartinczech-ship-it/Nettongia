OpenPDF Editor 0.18.0

This program uses PyMuPDF, PySide6, Pillow and the offline OCR engine exposed by
the pinned PyMuPDF/MuPDF build. The required OCR language models are bundled;
no Tesseract installation or network download is needed at runtime.
