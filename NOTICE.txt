Nettongia PDF Editor

This program uses PyMuPDF, PySide6, Pillow, pyHanko and the offline OCR engine
exposed by the pinned PyMuPDF/MuPDF build. The required OCR language models are
bundled;
no Tesseract installation or network download is needed at runtime.
Exact package license files are included in the installed `licenses` directory;
